No. 04Travel

Getting to
Nerja

AGP

Fly to Málaga

To get to Nerja, fly to Málaga Airport (AGP). Both Ryanair and Aer Lingus fly to Málaga from Knock, Shannon, Cork and Dublin.

KnockNOC → AGP
ShannonSNN → AGP
CorkORK → AGP
DublinDUB → AGP
50 min

Transfer or taxi

The easiest and quickest way to get to Nerja from Málaga Airport is to organise a transfer. They will ask for your flight number and hopefully group people together in a minibus to keep costs lower.

Always pre-order a taxi/transfer — if you try to get a taxi on arrival, drivers will charge much more and rarely know where they're going in Nerja.

By bus

Getting to Nerja by bus means first getting from Málaga Airport to Málaga city bus station. The buses are comfy and air-conditioned.

i
Airport → Málaga Bus StationA Express bus (Line 75) leaves from outside T3 arrivals. Every 20–30 min, 07:00–24:00 from the airport (06:25–23:30 to the airport). Journey 20–30 min.
ii
Buy your ALSA ticketAt Málaga Bus Station, buy a ticket at the ALSA office. The Nerja bus usually leaves from bay 38.
iii
Málaga → NerjaApprox. 1h25 on a normal bus, about 1h direct. Approx. €4.50 one way.
Málaga → NerjaALSA · Bay 38

No. 05Where to Stay

Around the
Balcón

We recommend staying in Nerja town centre, as the welcome party, ceremony and Day 2 all happen in Nerja. Using the Balcón de Europa as the centre point, there are lots of hotels, apartments, villas and Airbnbs within walking distance. If you're unsure about location, message us — we're happy to help!

A note on hostales

Hostales in Spain are more like B&Bs or guest houses — not shared accommodation as in Ireland.

Excellent value and comfortable, but without some hotel facilities (pool, gym, parking).
